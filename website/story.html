    THE WORKBENCH

    TURN A CLAIM
    INTO A CASE
    TO REVIEW.

    Start with the bill, then put its supporting record in context. Billing findings and provider evidence stay distinct so the investigator can see what happened, what is known, and what needs a person to decide.

    01

    REVIEW THE BILL

    Deterministic rules over the line items — repeated codes, units out of band, prices that do not match — and then a second, separate pass where a model reads the note against what was billed. This answers: how much should a person review this claim? Every finding keeps its source, so the investigator can follow the evidence back to the check that raised it.

    OUTPUT · REVIEW PRIORITY · LOW / REVIEW / HIGH

    02

    CHECK THE RECORD

    The workbench checks the federal registry and reports what corroborates the submitted record, what is missing, and what needs clarification. It never blocks intake. A gap in the record is a gap in evidence, never a judgment about the provider.

    OUTPUT · RECORD STATUS · CONFIRMED / PROBABLE / INCOMPLETE / CONFLICTING

    Where our own coverage is incomplete, the interface says so in the same colour it uses for itself — never in a warning colour. A gap on our side must not read as a mark against a provider. And when a provider disagrees with a finding, the case moves to a right of reply.
