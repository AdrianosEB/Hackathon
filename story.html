    A CODING ERROR IS NOT AN ACCUSATION.

    How much of this claim's own content needs a person to look at it, and how well the provider record matches the federal registry, are different questions. Answering them together is how one becomes the other. So we keep them apart.

    THE CLAIM

    Deterministic rules over the line items. Repeated codes, units that do not match the note, amounts out of band. Instant, repeatable, no model involved — and it never depends on who the provider is.

    02

    THE RECORD

    An agent panel queries the federal registry and reports what corroborates and what does not. Slow, fallible, and never allowed to block intake. A low score is a statement about the evidence, never about the provider.

    OUTPUT · DATA CONFIDENCE 0–100

    Where our own coverage is incomplete, the interface says so in the same colour it uses for itself — never in a warning colour. A gap on our side must not read as a mark against a provider.
